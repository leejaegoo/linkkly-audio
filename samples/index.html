Make AI Fit Human Behavior. AI-native products should adapt AI to human behavior while preserving clarity, control, learning, and useful non-AI interactions. The phrase “AI native” is popular, but its meaning is unclear. Many teams use it to show that AI is central to a product. This can lead them to add more AI even when older tools already work well. Using a technology often does not mean using it wisely. The article says teams should judge AI by the value it creates, not by how much of it appears in a product.
